(Video/out/Ampere-Intro.mp4 and Video/out/Ampere-Intro-4K.mp4)

into Video/out/music, and is refused unless its SHA-256 matches the one
below. The composer (Video/IntroVideo.swift, Music.known) is the source
of these entries.

What the license asks
  CC BY 4.0 allows copying, sharing, and adapting the music, including
  commercially, as long as the author is credited, in any reasonable
  manner for the medium. The composer prints the credit line above on
  the video's closing card, which is how a film carries it; a repeat in
  a video description is a courtesy. Kevin MacLeod's own wording is:

    Digital Lemonade Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)
    Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 License
    http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

  Incompetech also sells a Standard License for the same tracks that asks
  for no credit; with one bought, the credit lines in Music.known can go.

A track of your own (AMPERE_VIDEO_MUSIC=<file>) is mixed the same way but
credited by nobody: its license is yours to satisfy.
